# Privacy Policy – EyeDrive Monitor

EyeDrive Monitor does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

The app uses the device camera exclusively for real-time, on-device visual analysis.
No images or videos are recorded, saved, or transmitted to external servers.

All processing happens locally on the user’s device.
No personal information, identifiers, or usage data are collected.

The app does not use advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking services.
